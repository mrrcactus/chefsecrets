TITLE: Turkey Taco Lettuce Wraps
FROM: TikTok
SERVINGS: 3
MACROS PER SERVING: 330 kcal | 34 P | 12 C | 16 F
INGREDIENTS:
- 450 g | ground turkey | swaps: ground chicken, lean ground beef
- 1 packet | taco seasoning
- 1 head | romaine lettuce | swaps: butter lettuce, tortillas
- 1/2 cup | salsa
- 1/2 cup | shredded cheddar | swaps: feta
- 1 | avocado
STEPS:
1. Brown the turkey in a pan, breaking it up as it cooks.
2. Add the taco seasoning and a splash of water and simmer for 3 minutes.
3. Spoon into lettuce leaves and top with salsa, cheese and sliced avocado.

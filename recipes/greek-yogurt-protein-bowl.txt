TITLE: Greek Yogurt Protein Bowl
CATEGORY: Breakfast
FROM: Claude
SERVINGS: 1
MACROS PER SERVING: 410 kcal | 38 P | 45 C | 9 F
INGREDIENTS:
- 250 g | greek yogurt | swaps: skyr, cottage cheese
- 1/2 scoop | vanilla protein powder
- 1/2 cup | blueberries | swaps: strawberries, raspberries
- 30 g | granola | swaps: oats
- 1 tsp | honey | swaps: maple syrup
STEPS:
1. Stir the protein powder into the yogurt until smooth.
2. Top with berries, granola and a drizzle of honey.

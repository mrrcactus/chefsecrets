TITLE: Chicken Pesto Pasta
CATEGORY: Meal
FROM: ChatGPT
SERVINGS: 2
MACROS PER SERVING: 540 kcal | 45 P | 52 C | 15 F
INGREDIENTS:
- 250 g | chicken breast | swaps: turkey breast, firm tofu
- 140 g dry | penne | swaps: chickpea pasta, rotini
- 2 tbsp | pesto
- 1 cup | cherry tomato
- 1 cup | spinach | swaps: kale, arugula
- 1 tsp | olive oil
- to taste | salt
STEPS:
1. Boil the penne in salted water until al dente, then drain.
2. Cube and season the chicken, then cook in olive oil over medium-high heat for 6-7 minutes.
3. Add the halved tomatoes and spinach and cook until the spinach wilts.
4. Toss everything with the pesto and serve.
